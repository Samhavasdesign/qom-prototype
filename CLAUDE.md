# QOM product search prototype

A clickable, wireframe-level prototype of the Product Selection step in QOM (Waterworks' internal quoting web app). It exists to test layout and search behavior with sales consultants (SCs) before design handoff. It is not production code and is not Angular.

## Source of truth
- Requirements: the PRD "Improved Product Search & Results within a Quote" (Google Doc, QOM-2806; design epic QOM-2802, build epic QOM-2499). Requirement IDs (FR-1 to FR-21, NFR-1 to NFR-4) are marked in the code with `<span class="fr">` tags, which are always hidden on screen. `prd-trace.md` maps each requirement to the code.
- When the PRD and the prototype disagree, the PRD wins. Say so and propose a PRD change rather than silently diverging.

## File and stack
- `qom-product-search-prototype-v3.html`: the current prototype. One file, vanilla JS and CSS, no build step, no dependencies except Google Fonts (Libre Caslon Text, Figtree). It's v2 plus the step-track footer and the mocked quote tabs (see "What changed in v3"). `qom-product-search-prototype-v2.html` keeps the old two-row footer for comparison (Nov 6: it also got v3's mocked quote tabs, so both versions can be compared on every tab), and `qom-product-search-prototype.html` is v1, kept for reference.
- `bottom-bar-handoff/`: the brief (`PROMPT.md`) and reference images (00–15) that v3's footer and tabs were built from.
- Mock data only. Never add real client, quote or pricing data. Do not call QOM or M3.
- `images/`: eight sample product photos saved from the public waterworks.com wall-mounted accessories page, used as dummy thumbnails (not matched to the mock products). Products with no photo show a grey placeholder; a missing file falls back to the failed-image state.
- Open it directly in a browser, or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`).
- Breakpoints (Nov 9) match production (sp-fe docs/responsive-design.md): sm 640, md 768, lg 1024, xl 1280, 2xl 1536, defined once as `--bp-*` tokens on :root. Desktop-first: xl and up is the approved design, and narrower layouts use `(width < …)` queries with the name in a comment (CSS can't read the tokens inside @media). Below lg the rail becomes a black bar with a menu button and the wordmark, and the links open in a drawer (Escape, a click outside or a link closes it). That's a design proposal: production keeps the full side nav down to md. Search and quote stay side by side down to md and stack (no drag handle) below it. Container queries (panel widths) and the 480px header-icon gap are not mapped to these.

## Before you say it works
Extract the `<script>` contents and run `node --check` on them. One stray character in a template string breaks the entire script and the page goes inert.

## What is built (v2)
- Layout: search and quote side by side with a drag handle to resize them, plus an expand button for a full-screen quote view. The tabs layout (B) is hidden.
- Search: runs as you type, about a quarter second after typing stops, and Enter searches at once. This is the ideal case (Nick, Nov 5); the PRD's provisional decision is Enter only, so "Search as you type" in the demo bar switches to that fallback. Words match in any order and case. Exact item numbers and old 12-digit JDE codes come first, with light typo tolerance, and matches are underlined. Service parts are ranked below sellable products (and tagged "Service part"), not hidden.
- Filters: pills for Finish, Collection, Category (with product types beneath) and More filters (line type, plus "Hide discontinued and in-development items"), each opening a popover over the results. They work before typing, stay set for the quote until cleared, and show as black removable pills with "Clear all". Counts use the same rules as the results. Values that differ only by case or spacing ("Brass", "BRASS") are one option. Trays aren't one long scroll (Sam, Nov 6, in v2 and v3): Finish, Collection and More filters open 560px wide (capped to the search side). Finish has two panes: groups on the left (Most used, each family, Stone and tile colors for Surfaces), each with a black badge showing how many are ticked, and the chosen group's finishes on the right, each pane scrolling on its own. Collection and Line type lists run in two columns, filled top to bottom. Under 440px wide, lists drop to one column and the Finish groups become a row of chips. The old family accordions, "Show n more families" and the separate stone-colors view are gone. The Finish tray has no "Stays on for this quote" note; like the others, its footer shows only Clear, once something is ticked. Category is unchanged.
- States: an empty-search prompt, "keep typing" under three characters, loading, no results with next steps, and an error with retry.
- Can't-add reasons: discontinued (status 80 or 90) and in development (status 10) show in results with the reason. Warehouse status and "waiting on a price" only appear when the item is added. They show by default. The SC hides them with "Hide discontinued and in-development items" at the bottom of More filters (unticked by default, like every filter; it counts on the More filters badge and Clear all resets it), and the results count no longer has a Hide or Show note (removed Nov 5); "Show them" stays in the no-results state. Both stay in step with the demo bar toggle.
- Every result row shows the item's status: in the Status column when the search side is 500px or wider, otherwise in the line under the name ("Style ARTF · Status 20"). Kelli asked for it on Nov 5 because today's results show it.
- Results have no column header bar (Add, Picture, Name, Item Number, Status, Line Type, Unit) at any width; Sam removed it on Nov 5. The header row stays in the table for screen readers.
- Every result row has a "Details" button with a chevron, at every width, that opens a tray with the unit, weight, collection and finish (Nick, Nov 5; it used to open only from the unit column, which hides under 640px). Units read "each" and "sq ft", and the column is headed "Unit", not UOM, EA or SF.
- Country of Origin shows on quote lines only in the Commercial view (it's commercial-only in QOM).
- Result rows for sellable items (status 20) show a stock tag labelled with its warehouse: "In stock: 4 in USC", or "Out of stock in USC · lead time about 12 weeks". Sam asked for it back on Nov 5; it's a PRD change from the Oct 30 "no stock in results" decision, so it needs engineering's agreement on cost. Fuller availability appears on the quote line, worded as QOM-2926 will build it: "Available to Promise (ATP): 4" (or "9 from Feb 15, 2027" when only available later), "Estimated delivery date: Nov 12, 2026 · meets RDD" (or "· after RDD"), and "Sourcing warehouse: <code>" only when it differs from the line's warehouse (mock: Henry Single Robe Hook, HNHK-N, ships from USR). ATP and the date each have a two-layer tooltip: a plain sentence, then an "M3:" line (AVTP, CODZ, OIS340MI/LstSupplSummary, mock customer and quantity). No "as of" time.
- Room picker: the quote's own rooms come first, then "+ Add room", then the standard room types. One form adds or renames a room: "Rename room" in a room's "…" menu opens the Add room form filled in. Type is required and name is optional. The room bar shows the type in grey after the name ("sams kitchen · Kitchen") unless they're the same. The standard room types run in two columns, filled top to bottom, in v2 and v3 (Sam, Nov 6); long names ("Store Display Use Only") end in "…" with the full name on hover, and under 280px wide the dropdown goes back to one column. In v2 only (Nov 6): the dropdown runs down to 12px above the footer bar (`fitRoomMenu()`, never touching or covering it, at least 160px tall) instead of stopping at 360px, and when the sides are stacked it sits over the quote's pinned tabs bar instead of under it.
- Quote panel: rooms, lines, quantities, per-line shipping, "Find in this quote", plus Quote information and PDF preview tabs.
- Empty quote (Nov 6, v2 and v3, every tab and full screen): a carton icon (box with a label), "No products yet", "Add a room, then search for products to add to it." On Product Selection the button is Add room (opens the room form as before); on the other page tabs it's "Product selection", which goes to Product Selection and puts the cursor in search.
- Products tab dot: on Quote information or PDF preview, the Products tab shows a dot when lines need attention, with the same count and colours as a folded room's "needs attention" warning: red if any line blocks sending (wrong quantity, discontinued, stock short, status unavailable), amber if all are risks (the red is a brighter alert red, `--alert`, Nov 6). The page tabs above: Essential Quote Info, Header Information, Shipping Information and Associations are mocked in both v2 and v3 (same code; see "What changed in v3"); Admin: Calculations and Communications aren't built (hover state only). It's for problems only, not unsaved changes, which the footer already shows (Sam, Nov 6). Its tooltip and screen-reader label read "4 lines need attention".
- Removing a line: "Remove Product" in the line's "…" menu, or the stepper's trash at the minimum. The always-visible delete button under "…" (Nick and Vicki, Nov 5) was removed the same day as a duplicate (Sam). Either way opens a confirmation showing the product, quantity and room, with Cancel and "Yes, delete". Undo appears after the line goes.
- Reordering: drag a line or room by its handle, click the small up and down arrows above and below a line's handle (one click moves it one place; the stack is centred on the quantity stepper), use the arrow keys on the handle, or pick Move up or Move down at the top of a line's "…" menu (Nick, Nov 5). They're greyed out at the top and bottom of the room, and a move flashes the line and is announced to screen readers.
- Find in this quote: the usual find-in-page pattern, for items only (rooms are never matched). Every word must match the start of a word in the product name with its finish ("… in Nickel") or the start of the item number. After a pause or Enter it underlines every match and goes to the best one (exact item number first); the up and down arrows in the box, or Enter and Shift+Enter, then step through the matches one by one in quote order, wrapping at the ends, and the panel scrolls to each. The current line has a black outline. Small black text under the box says "3 of 21 matches" (or "No matching lines"), and each room bar shows "· 2 matches". Nothing is hidden.
- Quantities follow QOM-2491 (order multiples): items sold in multiples start at one multiple, + and − step by it, and the helper reads "Sold in increments of 10 square feet". A typed amount that isn't a whole multiple rounds up to the next one, with QOM-2961's note ("Rounded up to 30 square feet: this item is sold in increments of 10 square feet."), on result rows and quote lines. A saved quantity that's already wrong is flagged, not changed.
- Footer bar: save status with an "unsaved changes" marker, Save, Calculate freight, Send to client, and the "Before you send" panel.

## What changed in v3 (Nov 6)
- Step-track footer (`#pbar`): a 96px bar. On the left is the total block: "Estimated total" (or "Total" once freight is done), the serif amount, and the note · Full breakdown. On the right are three 200px steps, 1 Save, 2 Freight and 3 Send, each a label row (indicator, name, short status) over its 40px button, joined by connectors that turn black once the step before is done. The next step's button is filled black, other available steps are outlined, and disabled ones are #f3f3f3 with #666 text. Calculate freight is the next step only when nothing else blocks sending. While freight runs, a 3px line slides along the bottom of the button (static at 45% with reduced motion). "N to fix" (`#pbFix`) sits in the Send label. `#pbHelp` is now only a screen-reader live region. Ids, handlers, `footState()` and the panel's content are unchanged from v2.
- When it doesn't fit, `fitBar()` steps from full to tight (the note truncates) to compact (statuses hidden, and each button gets `aria-describedby` pointing at its status) to stack (total on row 1, three equal steps on row 2; on a phone the step numbers go). Collapsed (36px, the total, the step names without numbers, and only the next-step button), it hides the statuses, then the names, then the connectors, then the "Estimated total" label.
- "Before you send" no longer covers the bar. `placeBar()` ends the bar at the panel's left edge, full or collapsed. When less than 480px would be left (a phone), the panel covers the bar instead.
- Mocked page tabs: Essential Quote Info, Header Information, Shipping Information and Associations keep Product Selection's split: the form (copied from the staging screenshots, mock values for QUALITY FINISHES) takes the search side's place on the left, and the right is the same quote panel (Products, Quote information, PDF preview) at the same default width, with the drag handle between. A dragged width stays when you switch tabs. The old static Preview drawer is gone (Sam, Nov 6). "Add room" or "Search for products to add here" in the panel go back to Product Selection, where those live. Admin: Calculations and Communications still aren't built. There's no per-tab Save or Discard: the bar is the only Save. Field edits mark the quote unsaved, count in the bar, and revert with Discard. Delivery Address stays empty while "Delivery address missing" shows, and picking one of its two mock addresses clears the blocker. The panel's "Add address" opens Shipping Information with Delivery Address focused. Clicks on the page tabs and these forms don't close the panel. v2 has the same change (Nov 6).
- Finish split by category (Nov 6, from Kelli's and Josh's notes on tile color; PRD open question on tile color, design spec S-05, QOM-3218): a Surfaces product's finish is listed as a stone or tile color, every other product's as a metal finish. With no category picked, or Surfaces plus another category, "Stone and tile colors" is the last group in the Finish tray's left pane and lists them A–Z; with Category: Surfaces only that list shows. The Finish search finds a color by name ("bloom" finds Bloomsbury). Metal finishes no family covers sit last under "Other finishes". 14 mock "Architectonics 3 x 6 Tile" items (ARCH36-…) in different colors make it testable. v2 has the same filter code but not the 14 mock tile items, so it shows few or no stone colors. Washstands (a stone color and a metal finish on one item) aren't covered.

## Engineering constraints (from Scott and Imogen, Oct 27 to 30, 2026)
- Search runs on QOM's product table, not live M3. Cached fields: SKU, name, secondary description (FUDS), collection code, series, color, metal finish, UOM, category, item group, status, region, CHCD, SALE. Old JDE codes are in the table.
- Price needs an M3 call, so it is off in results by default (toggle in the demo bar).
- ATP and quantity are read in the context of the quote, not per search result. Full availability (ATP, delivery date) stays on the quote line; the stock tag on result rows (added back Nov 5) still has to be costed with Scott and Nick, from QOM's product table or one batched read, not an M3 call per row.
- Product status is known at search time. Item and warehouse status are known only when the item is added.
- Styles (CHCD = 2) and non-sales items (SALE = 0) are already excluded. Service parts are item groups starting "SVP".
- Minimum search length stays, with an info state instead of silence. Type-ahead is likely cut (the prototype shows it anyway as the ideal case, with a demo-bar toggle for Enter only).
- Thumbnails come from a separate imagery system and often fail; the prototype shows a failed-image state.

## Design principles
- Trust and clarity over speed: the SC always knows what saved, what didn't and why.
- Model what M3 stores; keep QOM-only data to a minimum.
- State requirements as outcomes. Mechanisms (chips, drawers, buttons) are design choices to test, not decisions.

## Open design questions
- One screen, tabs, or resizable split (test all three).
- Whether "stock after adding" satisfies SCs who want to browse by stock.
- Default for discontinued and in-development items (show or hide).
- Whether warehouse problems can be surfaced before the click.
- Whether filters combine as AND or OR (unknown; test with SCs).

## Style
Follows the QOM staging screens: black left rail with a spaced serif wordmark, serif quote header, uppercase black buttons, yellow Draft pill, grey table headers. Light theme only; dark mode was removed on request (colours are CSS variables on :root).
