A simpler way to build a quote

Building a quote today means moving between two rows of tabs: the quote's own tabs across the top (Essential Quote Info through Communications), and a second set inside the quote panel (Products, Quote information and PDF preview). This proposal removes both. The products on the quote stay in the middle of the screen, and the tools you need, product search and the quote's details, open beside them when you want them.

The page starts with creating a new quote, then shows the quote page itself, as it works today and as proposed. These are clickable mockups with sample data: try opening and closing the tools, searching the quote and previewing the PDF. Jump to the quote page.

Creating a new quote

Today a new quote opens on Essential Quote Info with an empty preview filling half the screen. After Create, about 13 more required fields wait across four tabs, with nothing saying which. Here, creating is one short step, QOM fills in what it can, and the SC goes straight to adding products. Whatever's still missing waits in Details and Things to fix.

Step 1 · Create

  • One centred form, about 560px. No tabs, no preview, no split: there's nothing to show beside it yet.
  • Only what M3 needs to create the quote, plus Project (optional), which lets Quote Description default to the project name.
  • "Filled in for you" shows each default openly, so nothing changes without the SC seeing it. All of them can be changed later in Details.
  • It could also open as a large dialog over the Quotes list, where Create new starts.

Step 2 · After Create

  • Lands in the proposed layout with Search open and the cursor in the box.
  • The empty lines area starts the quote: pick a room type (named "Primary Bath 1", and so on, as the room form does) or add your own. The layout doesn't move once products arrive.
  • The 4 fields still missing don't stop the SC adding products. They show as a red dot on Details and as the count on Send to client.

The quote page

Why the two rows feel wrong

The rows look the same but do different jobs. The page tabs say which part of the quote I'm editing. The panel tabs say how I'm looking at the whole quote. When two controls look alike and are stacked, people read them as a parent and child, and expect the lower row to change with the upper one. It doesn't, so they lose track of where they are. Two of the three panel tabs also aren't places to work:

  • Quote information is a read-only summary of the whole quote (dates, people, payment, fulfillment, documents). That's what the page tabs already cover, field by field. In the proposal, the few facts SCs need in view sit in the header instead.
  • PDF preview is an output, not a place to edit. Outputs are actions: a button that opens the document, and closes back to where you were.
  • Products is the only real content of the panel. In the proposal it becomes the middle of the page, with no tabs at all.

Folding the tool panel

  • One control. Click the open tool's icon again, or the ‹ in its header, to fold the panel. Click any tool to open it on that tool. Switching and folding are the same control, as in Figma or VS Code.
  • It replaces the expand button. Today the quote has a full-screen expand and a drag handle. A folded panel is the full-width quote, so the separate expand mode goes. The drag handle stays for in-between widths.
  • Always a way back. The tool column never folds, so Search is one click away. Keyboard: / opens Search with the cursor in the box.
  • Remember it per person. If an SC folds the panel to review the quote, it stays folded until they open a tool. Store it like the side nav's fold.
  • Two strips side by side. The app's black side nav and the white tool column must look clearly different, and the side nav should start folded to icons on quote pages. Don't fold the tool panel by default: search is the main job.
  • Accessibility. Each tool is a real button with aria-pressed and a name ("Search products", "Quote details, 4 fields needed before you send"), a 44px hit area, and the width change isn't animated under reduced motion.

Today the fields are split by M3's tabs, so one job is spread across several of them: Payer is on Header Information but Payer Contact is on Associations, and Delivery Address is on Shipping Information but Delivery Contact is on Associations. Open points before this goes in the prototype
